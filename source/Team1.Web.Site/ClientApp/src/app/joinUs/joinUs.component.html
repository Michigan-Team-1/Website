Join Team 1

Team-1 is Michigan's oldest, largest and most experienced High Power Rocketry club. Founded in 1988, we now have over 100 members, and it's those members that make Team-1 Michigan's premier rocketry group. Team-1 flies everything from the largest N powered rockets to the smallest Estes rockets and everything in between. We conduct well run, safe, and insured launches that have waivers from the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA).
